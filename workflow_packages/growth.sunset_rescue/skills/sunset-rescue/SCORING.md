# Scoring a forced-move event

## Deadline phase

Use today's UTC date, the cited deadline and, where given, the export end date.

| Phase | When | What users are doing | The play that fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| announcement | First 21 days after the announcement | Reading the news, searching "<tool> alternative", comparing | Landing page and honest comparison, answer the open threads |
| middle | After announcement, more than 30 days before the deadline | Most people wait | Make the import path easy, and answer questions as they appear |
| final_stretch | Last 30 days before the deadline | People who waited now have to move | Step-by-step move guide, a reminder of the export deadline |
| afterlife | Up to 90 days after the deadline | Latecomers realise their data or access is gone | Help recovering or converting old exports |
| closed | More than 90 days after the deadline, or export no longer possible | Moves are over | Do not build a kit; keep only for the record |

Searches and new threads tend to bunch up at the announcement and again near the deadline,
with a quiet stretch in between. Treat this as a pattern to check against the evidence you
found (for example the dates of the threads), not as a guarantee.

## Factors

Score each factor 0 to 3 and cite the evidence for the number.

- Displacement: how many people are forced to move. 0 means no sign of real users. 1 means
  a niche group. 2 means an active community or clear signs of many users. 3 means a widely
  used product or a vendor-stated large user base. Use vendor figures, community size or
  number of threads as evidence. Never make up a number.
- Fit: how well this product covers the three tasks those users rely on most. 0 means none
  of them. 1 means one task. 2 means two tasks, or all three with clear gaps. 3 means all
  three. Compare against the Station 1 tasks, and name the gaps.
- Portability: how easily their data comes across. 0 means no export exists or export has
  closed. 1 means an export exists but the product cannot use it and it is hard to convert.
  2 means a manual route or a small importer would work. 3 means the product already
  imports the format.
- Reachability: whether users are gathering in public places you can name. 0 means nowhere
  you can find. 1 means scattered. 2 means one or two active threads or forums. 3 means
  several active places with unanswered questions.

Phase weight: announcement 1.0, final_stretch 1.0, middle 0.7, afterlife 0.5, closed 0.

Score = (Displacement + Fit + Portability + Reachability) x phase weight, out of 12.

## Vetoes

A veto removes the event no matter what its score is. Record which veto applied.

- Fit is 0 or 1. Winning users you cannot serve only turns them into your own churn, one
  step later.
- The phase is closed.
- The event is unconfirmed, or the vendor has reversed it.
- The product is itself affected in the same way, for example it is built on the platform
  that is changing its terms.
- The only route to reach users would be contacting individuals, not public places.

## Choosing

Rank the surviving events by score. Break ties by the earliest upcoming deadline, then by
higher Portability. Choose one event. Every other surviving event goes on the watch list
with the date its next phase begins, which is when it is worth reconsidering.
