---
name: hiring-signal-prospects
description: Find and verify recent public hiring signals that indicate a potential need for the founder's offering, then turn them into an actionable prospect queue.
---

Use the founder's stated offering, ideal customer, target hiring roles, market, and freshness window.

1. Search public sources for companies in the stated market that posted or materially updated relevant hiring roles within the freshness window.
2. Treat a hiring post as a signal, not proof of buying intent. Prefer the company's own careers page or another primary source; use reputable secondary sources only to supplement or discover leads.
3. Verify the company fits the stated ideal customer before including it.
4. Read the relevant job description and identify the concrete operational problem or growth activity suggested by the role.
5. Connect that signal to the founder's offering. Do not invent pain points that the job description or other public evidence does not support.
6. Identify the most plausible buyer role based on the company and the signal. If uncertain, say so.
7. Produce a short list. For each prospect include:
   - company
   - hiring signal and approximate posting/update date
   - source/evidence
   - why the signal is relevant to the offering
   - likely buyer role
   - one specific next action
8. Exclude stale, unverifiable, duplicate, or weakly relevant signals. Do not fabricate contact details.
9. Write only the declared report. Do not contact prospects or change project state outside the declared output.

The report should distinguish observed facts from inference and should be concise enough for a founder to act on immediately.
