# Language

These rules hold for every paragraph. The project's own writing guide, when the package has
one, adds to them and wins where the two disagree.

## Sentences and paragraphs

- Paragraphs of one to three sentences. Each paragraph has one job.
- At most 25 words in a sentence. Vary the length within that limit.
- Active voice. Name who acts and what they act on.
- Keep the verb next to its direct object.
- Turn abstract nouns into verbs: "decide" for "make a decision", "check" for "perform a check".
- Use complete sentences. Keep articles and small linking words.
- Connect each sentence to the one before: carry a known subject or term forward. Use "because",
  "so" or "then" only when the relationship is real.
- One term for one thing. Keep product names exactly as the business writes them, and call a
  product by its name: never "the software", "the tool" or "the platform" in its place.

## Words

- Plain, literal words. No idioms, metaphors, slogans or wordplay.
- No em dashes or en dashes. Use a full stop, a comma or a colon.
- No contrast formulas: "not X but Y", "X, not Y", "it is not about X", "more than just X",
  "X isn't just Y", "rather than X". State the claim directly.
- No paired slogans ("Less X, more Y", "No X, no Y") and no closing aphorisms.
- No stock phrases of machine-written copy: "in today's fast-paced world", "delve", "unlock",
  "seamless", "game-changer", "navigate the landscape", "elevate", "empower", "harness",
  "it's worth noting", "at the end of the day", "the power of", "whether you're X or Y".
- No hedging chains ("can potentially help to"). Say what happens.
- No rhetorical questions in the body.

## Voice

- Direct, calm and conversational, written for a capable reader.
- Describe recognisable work through concrete actors and actions: the task, the place and the
  timing when the package supports them.
- Carry each explanation through to what someone can do or what changes in their work.
- Say what the business takes off the reader's hands. Mention the reader's own part only where
  they decide or approve.
