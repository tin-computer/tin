# People ranked by your ICP

A ranked list with the reason behind each score, a pick, and a record's panel.

Before: Hundreds of people react to your posts, and you can't tell which of them could buy.
After: Tin ranks them by your ideal customer, asks the ones who fit, and you start the conversation.

## 1. Your post
- Mark: post
- Caption: Your posts reach the right people. You just can't see who.
- Hero: the founder's LinkedIn post, inset top-left and off the foot.
- Satellite: "Who reacted", straddling the post's right edge from the first frame.
- Real words: Avery Lane; the post's two lines; 214 reactions · 31 comments; Who reacted; 214 people across your posts, last 90 days.
